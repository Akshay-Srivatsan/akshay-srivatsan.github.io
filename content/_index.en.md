# Akshay Srivatsan

## About Me

![An image of me]({{< link "portrait" >}})

I'm a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Stanford University,
advised by Professors [Keith Winstein]({{< link "keithw" >}}) and [Dawson Engler]({{< link "engler" >}}) and working in
the Systems and Networking Research Group at Stanford. My main research topic is
applying functional programming principles and ideas to (distributed) operating
systems.

In addition to my Ph.D. advisors, I've also had the privilege of being advised
by Professors [Frans Kaashoek]({{< link "kaashoek" >}}) and [Sara Achour]({{< link "sachour" >}}) on different projects.
